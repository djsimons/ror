Check the accident repair first

The math assumes the car is safe. After a major accident, have a shop check for frame or unibody damage and confirm the airbags were replaced. Uneven tire wear, a car that won't hold alignment, or doors and trunk that don't close right are warning signs. If it isn't safe, replace it whatever the numbers say.
